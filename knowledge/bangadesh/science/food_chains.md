# Food Chains

A food chain shows how food energy moves from one organism to another.

## Producers

Green plants are producers because they make their own food through photosynthesis.

They form the starting point of many food chains.

## Consumers

Consumers obtain energy by eating plants or other animals.

A primary consumer usually eats a producer.

A secondary consumer may eat a primary consumer.

## Example

Grass → grasshopper → frog → snake

The grass is the producer.

The grasshopper eats the grass.

The frog eats the grasshopper.

The snake eats the frog.

## Direction of arrows

The arrows in a food chain show the direction in which energy is transferred.

An arrow points from the organism being eaten toward the organism receiving the energy.

## Food webs

In real ecosystems, organisms often eat more than one type of food.

Several connected food chains form a food web.
