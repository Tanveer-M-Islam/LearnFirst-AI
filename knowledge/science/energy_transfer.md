# Energy Transfer

Energy transfer means energy moves from one object, organism or system to another.

Energy can also change from one form to another.

For example, a light bulb changes electrical energy mainly into light energy and thermal energy.

In ecosystems, much of the available energy begins with the Sun.

Plants capture light energy during photosynthesis and store chemical energy in glucose.

Animals receive chemical energy by eating plants or other animals.

Organisms use energy for movement, growth, repair and other life processes.

During energy transfers, some energy is released to the surroundings as heat.

Because organisms use energy and some energy becomes less useful to the next trophic level, less usable energy is normally available higher in a food chain.

Energy can change form, but energy is not created or destroyed.
