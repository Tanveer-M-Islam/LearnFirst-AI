# Ecosystems

An ecosystem includes living organisms and the non-living environment around them.

Living components include plants, animals and microorganisms.

Non-living components include water, air, soil, light and temperature.

Organisms interact with one another through feeding relationships, competition and cooperation.

Organisms also depend on environmental resources for survival and growth.

Because organisms are connected, a change in one population can affect other populations.

Pollution can affect many species because they share the same water, air, soil or food web.

Introducing or removing a species can also change population sizes throughout an ecosystem.
