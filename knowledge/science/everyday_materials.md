# Everyday Materials

Objects are made from materials such as wood, metal, plastic, glass, paper, rubber and fabric.

Materials have different properties.

A material may be hard or soft, flexible or rigid, transparent or opaque, absorbent or waterproof.

The properties of a material help determine how the material can be used.

Glass is useful for windows because it is transparent and allows light to pass through.

Rubber can be flexible and can provide useful grip.

Waterproof materials are useful for raincoats and umbrellas because they prevent water from passing through easily.

Metal is useful for many cooking pots because it is strong and can transfer heat.
