# Cells and Biological Systems

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

Multicellular organisms contain many cells.

Different types of cells can become specialized for particular jobs.

For example, muscle cells are specialized for contraction, while nerve cells are specialized for transmitting signals.

Groups of similar cells working together form tissues.

Different tissues working together form organs.

Different organs can work together to form organ systems.

For example:

cells → tissues → organs → organ systems → organism

Specialized cells allow complex organisms to perform many different functions efficiently.

Because organs in an organ system depend on one another, damage to one organ may affect the performance of the entire system.
