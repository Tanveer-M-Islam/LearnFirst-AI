# Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process plants use to produce glucose using light energy.

Plants require water and carbon dioxide for photosynthesis.

Chlorophyll in green plant cells absorbs light energy.

The energy is used to help produce glucose.

Oxygen is released during photosynthesis.

A simplified relationship is:

carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen

Light energy is required for this process.

Plants do not obtain their food directly from soil. Soil supplies water and mineral nutrients, while glucose is produced by photosynthesis.

If a plant receives too little light, the rate of photosynthesis can decrease and less glucose may be produced.

Photosynthesis is important because it provides chemical energy for plants, supports food chains and releases oxygen.
