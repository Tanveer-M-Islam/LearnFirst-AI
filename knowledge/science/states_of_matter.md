# States of Matter

Matter commonly exists as solids, liquids and gases.

In a solid, particles are close together and usually vibrate around fixed positions.

In a liquid, particles remain fairly close together but can move past one another.

In a gas, particles are much farther apart and move freely in different directions.

Melting occurs when a solid changes into a liquid.

Freezing occurs when a liquid changes into a solid.

Boiling or evaporation changes a liquid into a gas.

Condensation changes a gas into a liquid.

Heating generally gives particles more energy and makes them move faster.

Cooling removes energy from particles.
