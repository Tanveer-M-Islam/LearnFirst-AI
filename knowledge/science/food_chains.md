# Food Chains

A food chain shows how energy moves between organisms.

Most food-chain energy originally comes from the Sun.

Green plants are called producers because they make their own food through photosynthesis.

Animals obtain energy by eating plants or other animals.

In a food chain, arrows represent the direction of energy transfer.

For example:

Sun → grass → rabbit → fox

The grass captures energy from sunlight.

The rabbit receives energy by eating the grass.

The fox receives energy by eating the rabbit.

A change in one part of a food chain can affect other organisms. If grass decreases greatly, rabbits may have less food, and foxes may later have fewer rabbits to eat.
