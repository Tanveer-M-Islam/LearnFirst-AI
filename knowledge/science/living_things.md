# Living Things

Living things include plants, animals and microorganisms.

Living things need resources to survive. Animals generally need food, water and air. Plants need water, light, air and nutrients.

Living things grow and change. They use energy and respond to their surroundings.

Many living things reproduce and produce new organisms.

Movement alone does not prove that something is alive. A toy car can move, but it does not grow, reproduce or perform biological life processes.

A seed may look inactive, but under suitable conditions it can germinate and grow into a plant.
