# LearnFirst AI — teaching-first walkthrough

Use https://learnfirst-ai.vercel.app. Start a new Mathematics lesson.

1. Show the five-step lesson journey. Open the first theory card, explain the fraction diagram, and ask the companion “Explain more simply”.
2. Read the three ideas and worked examples. Click “I’m ready to try”.
3. On 1/5 + 2/5, deliberately enter 2/10. Explain that the tutor recognizes a likely denominator misconception.
4. Ask “I’m still confused” to progress from a clue to a connection, a different example, and the method. The final arithmetic stays with the learner.
5. Retry with 3/5. Continue through the understanding checks and adapted practice.
6. Show the independent check: no hints or companion input. Complete three fresh questions.
7. Review the first-attempt score and independent score. The first wrong answer remains wrong in the baseline despite a later correct retry.

Describe the product as a working teaching-policy demo with a built-in lesson companion. Do not claim a live language model, general-purpose chatbot, complete curriculum RAG, or proven learning gains. The initial score is after theory, not a pre-teaching baseline. Results are browser-local; the original backend remains a separate learning project.
